# Company Review Policy

- Review the real diff and current repository rules.
- Report actionable Blockers before style preferences.
- Do not mix unrelated work into the reviewed scope.
- Do not claim a command, test or manual check ran without evidence.
- Review does not execute `git add`, `git commit`, `git push`, destructive
  cleanup or history rewriting.
- A clean Review cannot replace required Self Test or manual verification.
